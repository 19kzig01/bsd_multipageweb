About Us

Our mission and values

Who are we?

We are a vegan non-profit animal rescue organization and sanctuary in Ontario, Canada. We rescue and rehabilitate farm animals from slaughterhouses, laboratories, fur farms, and the entertainment industry by providing lifelong care, where they are able to live the remainder of their lives with respect, dignity, and freedom. We believe that every sentient being has a right to live a life free of suffering, oppression, and human exploitation.

We envision a peaceful world in which all sentient beings are treated with respect, kindness, and compassion. We believe that animal rights and other social justice movements are intersectional, and hold regular workshops and events to educate the public on the plight of animals used in different industries.
